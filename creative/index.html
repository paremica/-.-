About Me

My spirit animal is the cockroach, resilient, adaptable, and impossible to get rid of. I'm a cyberpunk enthusiast, a self-declared multifunctional neurotic, and someone with absolutely zero editing experience. My favorite game is Tetris, and I have a soft spot for apples with cream. When sadness hits, I retreat into Come and See, my cinematic safe haven. I'm deeply drawn to alternative music and have a habit of falling for people who pour their souls into their own independent sounds. And when it comes to books, Clemencia holds a special place, not because it's comforting, but because it reminds me that I am very much alone and that no one will ever love me quite like Fernando Valle.
