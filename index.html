Percolation Threshold Detection

+

Detected the percolation threshold using image classification algorithms by simulating circular particles in a 2D space and classifying images into "percolates" and "does not percolate" categories.

Trained an image detection algorithm with the Prophet library and validated against scanning electron microscopy images.

Advisor · José Ignacio Huertas Cardozo

Gemma Multi-device

+

Developed a device combining luminous, voltaic and thermal energy using fiber optics, semi-transparent PV modules and a Fresnel-based heat exchanger.

Programmed an Arduino light tracker and used COMSOL Multiphysics 6.0 to optimize focal performance. Principal competitor in XIGNUX Challenge 2023 and presenter in EXPOINGENIERÍAS 2023.

Dynamic Load Transfer Systems

+

Dynamic load transfer systems use advanced algorithms, real-time monitoring, and artificial intelligence to manage and transfer loads between multiple power sources and breakers seamlessly. These systems ensure uninterrupted power during maintenance activities by dynamically balancing loads based on real-time data and predictive analytics.

Summer Innovation Challenge · Schneider

iSolenum Solar Stove

+

A thorough evaluation and redesign of the iSolenum solar stove, enhancing utility for communities in Chiapas, Mexico. The proposed innovation leverages a heliodome to concentrate solar radiation, generating adequate thermal energy for cooking, a sustainable alternative to wood-burning stoves rooted in thermodynamics and solar energy principles.
